PPC as a Ranking Tool — Organic Plateau Context

Organic growth ran strongly from Jun '25 through Jan '26 — unit sales climbed from 45 to 92. Since then, monthly units have held in the 62–68 range. This kind of plateau after a growth run is common and typically reflects the listing reaching the ceiling of its current organic rank on key terms. PPC can play a direct role in breaking through that ceiling by generating conversion velocity on specific keywords, which signals Amazon to move the organic rank up.

How PPC drives organic rank movement
Amazon's A9 algorithm weights recent sales velocity per keyword when determining organic rank. A PPC conversion on "migraine supplement" tells Amazon that buyers searching that term purchased MiGuard — which improves the organic rank for that exact term over time. This is why high-spend months like Jan '26 ($643 spend, 22 PPC orders) often precede organic rank improvements that show up 2–4 weeks later in the keyword tracker.

The organic plateau since Feb '26 corresponds to a period of declining PPC orders (13 → 9 → 3). This doesn't mean PPC caused the plateau — but it does mean PPC isn't currently supplying the conversion velocity needed to push key terms higher.
